Privacy notice

This page says what information we hold, why, and what your rights are. It is written to match what this website and our service actually do — nothing here is boilerplate, and we collect very little.

Keelson Holdings Ltd is the data controller for personal data collected through this website. You can reach us about anything on this page at info@shipitstudio.co.uk.

Visiting this website

This site has no accounts and no login, and it sets no cookies. Three things happen in the background:

  • Hosting. The site is served by Vercel, whose servers keep standard request logs (your network address and the pages requested) for security and operations.
  • Analytics. We use Vercel Web Analytics, which counts page views in aggregate without cookies. We see how many people visited, not who you are.
  • Fonts. The typefaces load from Google Fonts, so your browser requests them from Google's servers, which see your network address in that request.

If you contact us

The "Join the pilot" buttons open a short enquiry form on the page. What you type there (your name, email address, phone number if you give one, and your message) is delivered to our inbox by Web3Forms, a form delivery service. You can also email us directly. Either way, we hold what you send in order to answer you and follow the conversation up. The lawful basis is legitimate interests: you wrote to us about the service. We do not add you to any mailing list, because we do not have one, and we will delete the correspondence if you ask.

The open quote check and Desk

Both tools run entirely in your own browser. The quotes, customer names and email addresses you type are not sent to us or to anyone else. We cannot see them.

  • The open quote check keeps nothing. Close the page and what you typed is gone.
  • Desk saves your list in your browser's own storage on your device, so it is still there next time. You can remove a quote in Desk, or clear the whole list by clearing this site's data in your browser.
  • Backups are text that you copy and keep yourself. We never receive them.
  • "Open in my email" hands the draft to your own email app, which sends it from your own account. We are not involved in sending it.

Because that information never reaches us, you remain responsible for your customers' details, exactly as you are when you email them yourself.

When we send follow-ups for a business

In the pilot, we send quote follow-ups on behalf of client businesses. To do that the client gives us a list of open quotes: typically a customer's name, email address, what the quote was for, its value and the date it was sent. That information belongs to the client's relationship with their customer:

  • The client is the data controller; Keelson Holdings Ltd acts as a processor, on the client's documented instructions, under a written agreement.
  • We only contact people who asked that business for a quote and were sent one. The service does not send cold email.
  • Every follow-up says which business it is from, and stops as soon as the customer replies or asks us to stop.
  • The information is used only to follow up that client's quotes, never for our own marketing, and is returned or deleted in line with the client agreement when the engagement ends.

We use a small number of suppliers to run the service, such as hosting and email delivery. Each acts as a sub-processor under contract, and we name the specific providers in each client agreement, or on request.

If you received a follow-up about a quote

That email was sent because you asked one of our clients for a quote. That business is the controller of your details, so questions are best sent to them. You can also reply to the email asking us to stop, or write to us at info@shipitstudio.co.uk, and we will stop the follow-ups and tell the business.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, object to our processing, and ask us to restrict it. Email info@shipitstudio.co.uk and we will respond within a month. If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
