The problem with enquiries that wait

Enquiries land while your fitters are on site and your office is busy. By the time someone rings back, the customer has a visit booked with a competitor. You paid for the advert or the lead either way.

What makes a home improvement lead worth a visit

You do not want to drive an hour to price a job that was never real. Before a visit most firms want the postcode, what the job is, whether the person owns the property, and when they want it done.

What Sales Chaser does

  • Rings each enquiry within a minute, at any hour you allow.
  • Confirms the job, the postcode and that it is in your area.
  • Checks ownership and timing using your questions.
  • Books the quote visit into your diary.
  • Retries no-answers and tells you who was not a fit, and why.

A worked example

A Saturday enquiry

A homeowner asks about replacing eight windows at 2pm on a Saturday. Sales Chaser rings at 2:01pm, confirms the address is in your area and that they want the work before Christmas, and books a visit for Tuesday at 5pm. This is an illustration, not a real customer.

How it works

  1. You send opted-in leads. People who have already asked to hear from you, plus your script and what a good lead looks like.
  2. The AI calls in seconds. Every new lead is rung within a minute, day or night. It follows your script, answers questions and checks the things you care about.
  3. Interested buyers go to your team. It books them straight into your sales team's calendar.

Common questions

Does it work with leads from our own website as well as bought leads?

Yes. Any lead where the person has asked to be contacted.

Are these cold calls?

No. We only call leads that have already opted in: people who enquired and expect to hear from you. The AI can confirm where they enquired at the start of the call.

What counts as a qualified lead?

You decide: budget, timeframe, location, product fit, whatever matters to you. We only bill for leads that meet it.

Does it sound like a robot?

It sounds natural, follows your script and handles questions. If a lead wants a person, it books them into your team's calendar.
