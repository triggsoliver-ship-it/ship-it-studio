Questions

The things everyone asks.

Do I need to change the software I use?

No. We build around the tools you already run — your inbox, CRM, accounting software, calendar. The automation slots into your current setup.

Is this just ChatGPT?

No. We use AI where it's the right tool (reading and writing messages, extracting data), combined with proper automation plumbing so things actually happen — reliably, every time, without someone pressing a button.

What if it makes a mistake?

We build in checkpoints where it matters — an automation can flag something for a human to approve before it sends. You decide how much runs fully hands-off.

How is it fixed price if every business is different?

That's what the free audit is for. Once we've seen the task, we can quote a fixed price for that specific build — so you know the number before you commit.

Who owns what you build?

You do. On a single build, it's yours. The retainer is for ongoing new automations and tuning — cancel monthly, keep everything already built.
