What retyping really costs

It is slow and it goes wrong. A wrong digit in a phone number loses a lead. A missed field breaks a report. And the person doing it was hired to do something more useful.

Where the details come from

Website forms, enquiry emails, booking systems, call notes and spreadsheets from partners. If your team reads it and types it somewhere else, it can usually be handed over.

What Offload does

  • Picks out names, contact details and the facts you track from emails and forms.
  • Creates or updates the record in your CRM or spreadsheet.
  • Checks for duplicates before adding anyone twice.
  • Flags anything it is unsure of for a person to confirm.
  • Keeps a log of what it added and where it came from.

A worked example

Fifty enquiries a week

A firm gets fifty web enquiries a week and types each into its CRM by hand, about four minutes each. With the automation the record is there before anyone opens the email. This is an illustration, not a real customer.
