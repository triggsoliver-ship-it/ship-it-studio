Built in stages, run side by side with the current system until it's trusted

How we'd build it

Stage 1

The core

  • Vans, drivers and who's in what
  • Central job board
  • Driver phone app with photos and signatures
  • Prices worked out from rate cards
  • End-of-day report
Stage 2

Customers & money

  • Customers book and track online
  • Ready-to-invoice checks, then invoices into the accounts software
  • Chasing with your approval, promises, part payments and disputes
  • Driver pay worked out, with exceptions for review
Stage 3

Grow

  • Regular routes set up once, repeated automatically
  • Parcel companies send jobs in bulk
  • Van use and profit reports
  • Route planning for multi-drop days

Questions to shape stage 1

  • How do jobs come in today, and who decides which van goes?
  • What happens outside the current system (spreadsheets, WhatsApp, paper)?
  • How are prices and driver pay worked out?
  • What would you most like to see at 6pm every day?

Why this matters

  • "People don't mind if they can see where you are." The link goes out the moment a job is booked.
  • It stops the calls asking where the driver is, which is most of what the office does today.
  • It carries your branding, so customers see London Delivery Network, not a courier app.
  • It never shows prices, balances, signatures or driver pay, and one customer can't see another's jobs.
