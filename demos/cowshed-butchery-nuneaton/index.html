Butcher & farm shop · Nuneaton, Warwickshire

Reared here. Hung here. Cut here.

Gorse Farm Shop has sold local and home-grown produce since the 1970s. Since we added The Cowshed Butchery in 2019 we’ve employed two full-time skilled butchers, Michael and John, so every piece of meat is hung and butchered traditionally on site — not delivered in boxes.
