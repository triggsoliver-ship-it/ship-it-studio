Demo theme styleguide

Eight themes. Every one has its own font pairing, its own hero structure (not a restyle of the same block), its own grid, corner radius and section rhythm.

The rule: no two demos built in the same batch use the same theme, and the theme is chosen to suit the trade — a tree surgeon and a nail studio should never look related.
